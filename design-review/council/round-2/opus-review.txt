**Overall: 7.28 out of 10.** The app is competent: the shell is consistent and the page structure is easy to read. It is not yet polished craft. Most deductions come from components that do the same job in different ways across screens, not from layout failures. That fits a product whose features were built separately before one shell was put around them.

The fastest gain is to standardize the shared pieces: one secondary button style, one way to switch or filter views, one timestamp format, and one style for the line of status details under each page title. That one change should lift design coherence, cleanliness, interaction affordance and spacing together.

Mobile is the weakest real-use area, and the icon-only red Clear button on Logs is the most consequential single defect.

**What I separated as fixture limits, not defects:**
- On Quota, the four accounts overlap as one line because every fixture account sits at 62%.
- The Dashboard chart shows zero traffic.
- The Usage "by day" charts are empty.
- The Plugins and Plugin Store pages have no plugins.
- The three Top Models rows on Usage are identical.

I scored only how the app handles those cases, such as its combined "1–4" label or its empty-state wording.

**What would raise the score most:**
- Data visualization: show empty charts as a short notice instead of a flat chart, and lead legends with a color swatch rather than a number.
- Information hierarchy: make the Auth Files rows denser so more credentials fit on one screen.
