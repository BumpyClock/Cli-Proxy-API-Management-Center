**Notes**

- **Fixtures.** I did not count these against the scores: the 4 identical 62% series, empty request flow, empty plugin lists, "Not reported" daily usage, Oct 20 log timestamps and "Build Time Unknown". Both the chart score and the Dashboard hierarchy finding rest on problems that remain with any data.
- **Claim I dropped.** I first suspected the Auth Files tabs had no active underline. Pixel sampling shows the same 2px #0B7285 underline as Logs, so it is not listed.
- **Contrast check.** The disabled Save measured 4.4:1, white on #77797C. Disabled controls are exempt from WCAG contrast, so I scored this as an affordance problem, not an accessibility failure.
- **Quota bar colour.** The amber 62% bar is intentional: QuotaGlanceList.tsx maps remaining quota to high, medium or low tones. My finding is the missing explanation of those tones. The threshold itself is a preference.
- **Overall level.** The product is a competent, coherent console at about 7.5. Reaching 9 mainly needs a consistent header-action pattern, a chart that actually encodes series and projection, a Dashboard that states health first, and the mobile action-placement fixes above. These changes are about structure and consistency, not decoration.
